How much does field service software cost in 2026?

The short answer: published prices run from $29 to $299 a month — and two of the biggest names publish no prices at all. Every figure below is verified, with the check date.

Field service software costs from $29 to $299 a month at the vendors that publish prices — and Workiz and ServiceTitan publish no prices at all. Prices checked 2026-11-11.

The short answer

Jobber publishes prices from $29 a month (Core, billed annually, 1 user). Housecall Pro publishes prices from $59 a month (Basic, billed annually, 1 user). Workiz and ServiceTitan publish no prices: their pricing pages show "Request pricing" for every plan. That is the whole honest picture at the top level — everything else is detail.

What drives the price?

Three things: users, tiers and add-ons. Most plans are priced per user or per bundle of users — Jobber charges $29 a month per additional user beyond the bundle, Housecall Pro $35 a month per additional user on MAX. The tier decides the features: inventory sits on Workiz's top Ultimate tier; QuickBooks sync starts at Housecall Pro Essentials and Jobber Connect. And the add-ons — phone systems and AI answering — are usually sold separately, with no published price: Workiz's Genius Answering, Housecall Pro's CSR AI and ServiceTitan's AI Voice Agent all have no published price. Jobber is the exception: its Receptionist is $29 a month, including 30 conversations, then $0.79 per additional conversation, with unlimited usage on the Plus plan.

The hidden costs

The monthly subscription is rarely the whole bill. Implementation is the first hidden cost: ServiceTitan's is a formal project with annual contracts, and third-party reports put its one-time implementation fee anywhere from $5,000 to $50,000 or more — an unverified estimate, not a ServiceTitan figure, and not used as a fact here. Per-user fees are the second: a $59 plan for one user is a different price once the second technician joins. Payment processing is the third: card processing runs from 2.59% at Housecall Pro to 2.9% + 30¢ at Jobber, and bank (ACH) payments are 1% at both. Ask for all three numbers before you compare two prices.

One real bill, with attribution

A New York appliance-repair competitor pays $700/month for Workiz with telephony — from the competitor's own words, not from Workiz's price list. Workiz publishes no prices, so this is one business's actual bill, not a Workiz price. It is the only cost datapoint of its kind in this article, and it is here because it is attributed, not because it is a price list. Third-party Workiz prices circulating online range from $19 to $325 a month with incompatible plan names, and are not used as facts.
