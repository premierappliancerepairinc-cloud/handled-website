Why repairmen miss calls

It is not carelessness. You are inside a machine, or driving between jobs, or holding a part with both hands. The phone rings in your pocket and you cannot take it. By the time you wipe your hands and call back, the customer has already called the next name on Google — and that repairman answered.

Voicemail does not fix this. A message says "call me back", and the customer hears "wait". A customer with a fridge full of food does not want to wait. They want a time.

What a dispatcher should do

Answering the phone is the easy half. The hard half is what happens after the hello:

  • Take the details — what is wrong, which appliance, the address, the phone number.
  • Agree on a time — a real appointment, not "someone will call you back".
  • Put it in the schedule — so the job exists before you even look at your phone.
  • Keep the client — a card with everything, so the next call starts where this one ended.

A message on your voicemail does none of that. An answering service that only takes messages does none of that either. The call is answered, but the job still waits for your evening.

How Sofia does it

Sofia is an AI voice dispatcher built into Dana. When you do not pick up, Sofia answers the call for you. You set her up once: record your own greeting, choose a voice, and choose after how many rings Sofia picks up — plus more settings. After that, she answers like your dispatcher, not like a robot reading a script.

And because Sofia lives inside Dana, the call does not end with a message. Dana listens to the call and creates a client card right away — what is wrong, which appliance, the address, the phone. You agree on a time, and Dana creates the appointment for that time. When you get to the customer, photograph the model number and Dana puts it in the card itself, helps with the diagnostics and finds the part.

What the big tools do

Checked 2026-11-11. Workiz Genius Answering, Housecall Pro CSR AI, Jobber Receptionist and ServiceTitan's AI Voice Agent all answer inbound calls, capture the caller's details and book jobs into the schedule. They are good at the job they do: no missed call, no voicemail to return. But they book calls; they do not run the app. The business behind the call — the schedule, the invoice, the van stock, the part — is still yours to do.

On price: at Jobber, Receptionist is $29 a month. At Workiz, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan the price of AI answering is not published.

What good looks like

  • The phone is answered in your words, every time — not just when you are free.
  • The caller leaves with a time, not a promise of a callback.
  • The appointment is in the schedule before you touch your phone.
  • The client card already exists when you arrive — with the appliance, the address and the problem.
  • Nothing about the call waits for your evening.
