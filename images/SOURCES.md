## Gaps found — stated plainly, not papered over

1. **No true in-home large-appliance repair photo.** Pexels has no
   freely-licensed photo of a technician repairing a refrigerator, washer,
   dryer, dishwasher or oven inside a customer's home. The repair shots
   above are workshop-based (the best honestly available). The many
   in-home fridge/washer repair photos found via general image search
   come from repair-company blogs and are mostly iStock/Getty images of
   unclear licence — they were **not** used, to avoid a copyright risk.
   Recommendation: commission a short real photo shoot (or use Badma's
   own on-the-job photos) for the hero if an in-home repair shot is
   wanted.
2. **No technician-with-customer photo.** No suitable free-stock photo of
   a smiling appliance technician with a customer was found: Pexels'
   "repairman smiling" results are all auto mechanics, and the
   delivery-driver photos that look friendly carry a large "DELIVERY"
   shirt logo and parcels. `technician-with-van-01.jpg` is the friendly
   technician portrait instead. Do not substitute a delivery/auto photo.
3. **Rejected on inspection (not used):** photos with Cyrillic text or
   Russian company logos on uniforms/signs (e.g. Pexels 34734504,
   37174867, 37492281), photos of the wrong subject (hard-drive repair,
   coffee machines, boilers, industrial motors, pressure washers,
   delivery parcels), photos with readable foreign licence plates or
   rundown non-US settings, and one AI-generated-looking doorway scene.
   Unsplash and Pixabay could not be used — their pages are blocked by
   policy in this environment (Unsplash returned 401, Pixabay blocked).
