What is Reserve with Google?

Reserve with Google puts a booking button on your business in Google Search and Maps. A customer searches for an appliance repair service, finds your business, and books a visit — without going to your website, without calling, without waiting for a call back. The booking goes straight into your schedule.

How does it work in Handled?

  1. The customer books on Google

    They pick a service and a time, right in Search or Maps.

  2. The job lands in the schedule

    The booking flows into Handled's schedule, and Max has the job, the client and the time.

  3. You get a booked job, not a missed call

    No phone tag, no voicemail to return, no lead that cools while you are under a sink.

Why does it matter?

Most customers book the first business that makes booking easy. Every extra step — a website to open, a number to call, a voicemail to leave — loses some of them. A booking button on Google removes the steps between "I need a repair" and "the repair is booked". For a one-person business, it also removes the missed call: the job is booked while you are on another job, not lost because you could not answer the phone.

An honest note on status: Reserve with Google needs a Google partnership, which is a long track. It lives on the Roadmap, which is the status page. This page describes the product as it will be; the Roadmap is the status page. The paid subscription starts when the Reserve with Google button works — see Pricing.

Reserve with Google and the competitors

Reserve with Google is verified at Workiz and Housecall Pro — both list it on their own pages. For Jobber and ServiceTitan it was not verified on the vendor pages checked on 2026-11-11, so this site does not assert that they lack it. Handled includes it; that is the claim, stated plainly.
