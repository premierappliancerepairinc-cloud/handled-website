Handled — AI CRM

Voice control for your whole business.

Speak, and the app does it. Voice control in Handled works on every tab — no chat to open, no typing, no menus to dig through.

Handled is controlled by voice on every tab. You speak, the app does it — no chat, no typing, no menus.

How does voice control work?

You talk to the app the way you would talk to a good dispatcher. Say it once, and Max does it: the job is booked, the schedule is updated, the invoice is drafted, the client gets a message back. You stay on the repair.

What can you say?

  • Book a job — "Book Mrs. Alvarez for Thursday morning, dishwasher not draining."
  • Check the schedule — "What do I have tomorrow?"
  • Find a part — "Find the drain pump for this LG washer."
  • Write an invoice — "Invoice the dryer repair, parts and labor."
  • Call the client back — "Text the customer I am on my way."

Why it matters on the job

A technician's hands are busy. You are under a sink, inside a panel, on a ladder, or driving between jobs. Typing on a phone is the slowest part of the day — and the part that gets skipped, so the paperwork piles up for the evening. Voice control removes that step. The job is recorded while you are still doing it, not after.

Voice works in the app in eight languages, so the technician who does the work can speak the language they think in.

Sofia, the voice dispatcher

Sofia is an AI voice dispatcher built into Max. When you do not pick up, Sofia answers the call for you. You record your own greeting, choose a voice, and choose after how many rings Sofia picks up — plus more settings.

How is this different from Jobber's AI Voice?

Jobber's AI Voice is the nearest thing any competitor offers — and it is a real feature. It lets a technician create quotes, update schedules, send messages and log notes hands-free in the mobile app. The difference is scope: Jobber's voice is for field tasks inside the mobile app. Handled's voice control runs the whole app, on any tab — the CRM, the schedule, inventory, parts, invoices and client records. You do not switch to a voice mode; the whole app listens. Features checked 2026-11-11.
