Handled — AI CRM

Handled vs Housecall Pro.

Housecall Pro is the all-in-one for small shops, with published prices and a 14-day trial. Handled is the AI agent that runs the business — with van inventory and parts search Housecall Pro does not have.

Handled is an AI CRM for small service businesses. Against Housecall Pro, its clearest edges are van inventory, parts search across stores, and a client card that grows itself.

The short verdict

Housecall Pro is the most complete off-the-shelf option for a small shop: online booking, scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments and review management, with published prices from $59 a month and a 14-day trial of the MAX plan. Handled's case is the gap Housecall Pro leaves open: no native van-stock inventory at all, nothing that finds parts across stores or drafts orders, and no diagnostics from manuals or photo part ID. Housecall Pro's AI answers calls and drafts; Max runs the CRM end-to-end.

Where Handled is different

  • Van inventory — Housecall Pro has no native van-stock inventory at all. This is Handled's clearest gap against Housecall Pro.
  • Parts search across stores with retail and wholesale prices, and draft orders — nothing comparable was found at Housecall Pro.
  • Voice control of the whole app — Housecall Pro's voice AI answers incoming calls; Handled lets the owner run the app by voice.
  • Diagnostics from real manuals, with confirmed/unconfirmed labels, and photo part ID — nothing comparable was found.
  • Client card that grows itself — Housecall Pro's records are built by manual entry.

Where the competitor is strong

  • Published prices and a 14-day free trial of the MAX plan, with no credit card required and no long-term contract.
  • The most complete small-shop package in one system: booking, scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments, financing and review management.
  • CSR AI, an AI receptionist that books jobs from inbound calls, plus an AI Team across the product.
  • A price book and job costing from the Basic plan, and a mobile app with offline viewing.
