Handled vs Jobber.

Jobber is the most transparently priced of the big four, and its AI Voice is the nearest thing to Handled's voice control. The difference is scope: Jobber's voice covers field tasks; Handled's voice runs the whole app.

Handled is an AI CRM for small service businesses. Against Jobber, its edges are voice control of the whole app, diagnostics from real manuals, and a client card that grows itself.

The short verdict

Jobber is the easiest of the four to adopt and the cheapest to start: published prices from $29 a month, a 14-day trial, and AI included on all plans. Its AI Voice is genuinely good and genuinely close to Handled — the nearest any competitor comes. Handled's case is scope: Jobber's voice is for field tasks in the mobile app; Handled's voice runs the whole app on any tab. Jobber also has no van-stock inventory, no cross-store parts search, no diagnostics from manuals and no photo part ID.

Where Handled is different

  • Voice control of the whole app — Jobber's AI Voice covers field tasks in the mobile app; Handled's voice runs the whole app, on any tab. The scope difference, stated fairly, is the point.
  • Van inventory — Jobber has no van-stock module. Its Home Depot lookup is a price check for quoting, not stock control.
  • Parts search across stores with retail and wholesale prices, and draft orders — Jobber's lookup is retail-only and Home Depot-only, with no ordering.
  • Diagnostics from real manuals, with confirmed/unconfirmed labels, and photo part ID — nothing comparable was found at Jobber.
  • Client card that grows itself — Jobber's records are built by manual entry.

Where the competitor is strong

  • Price transparency: the cheapest entry point of the four, with every plan and billing option published.
  • AI Voice — the nearest thing to Handled's voice control, and genuinely hands-free for field tasks.
  • AI included on all plans, including AI Chat, auto-draft quotes and AI-generated reports.
  • A client hub, quote add-ons, job costing, and a Home Depot catalog lookup for quoting — practical tools for a small shop.
