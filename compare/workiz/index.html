Handled — AI CRM

Handled vs Workiz.

Workiz is built around the phone — calls, texts and AI answering. Handled is built around Max, an agent that runs the whole business by voice. Here is the fair comparison.

Handled is an AI CRM for small service businesses. Against Workiz, its edges are voice control of the whole app, diagnostics from real manuals, and a client card that grows itself.

The short verdict

Workiz is the strongest of the four competitors on the phone: a built-in phone system, call tracking and recording, and Genius Answering that books jobs from missed calls. If your business lives or dies by inbound calls, Workiz is a serious product. Handled's case is different: one agent that runs the CRM, the van stock, the parts search and the diagnostics — by voice, on any tab — and a client card that builds itself. Workiz has real inventory, but only on its top tier behind a quote; it has no diagnostics from manuals and no photo part ID.

Where Handled is different

  • Voice control of the whole app — Workiz answers calls with AI; Handled lets the owner run the app itself by voice, on any tab.
  • Diagnostics from real manuals, with confirmed/unconfirmed labels — nothing comparable was found at Workiz.
  • Photo part ID — nothing comparable was found at Workiz.
  • Client card that grows itself — Workiz's equipment tracking is the closest analogue, but it is manual record-keeping.
  • Parts search across stores with retail and wholesale prices, and draft orders — Workiz tracks stock on its top tier; it does not search stores for parts.

Where the competitor is strong

  • The phone system: calls, texts and voicemail tied to the client record, with tracking and recording — the centre of the product, not an add-on.
  • Genius Answering: an AI dispatcher that books or reschedules jobs from calls, emails and SMS, around the clock.
  • Real inventory and purchase orders on the Ultimate tier — the closest inventory match among the four competitors.
  • Reserve with Google, a branded client portal and QuickBooks sync — parity with Handled on all of these.

Price

Workiz publishes no prices. Its pricing page lists three plans — Standard, Pro and Ultimate — and every plan shows "Request pricing", with no dollar amount for any of them. The page's FAQ states a 7-day free trial with no credit card required, and that annual subscriptions are discounted ("save $400+"), without giving the discounted prices. Handled's price is also TBA, so no honest price comparison can be made yet.

One cost datapoint, with full attribution: a New York appliance-repair competitor pays $700/month for Workiz with telephony — from the competitor's own words, not from Workiz's price list. Workiz publishes no prices, so this is one business's actual bill, not a Workiz price. Third-party Workiz prices circulating online conflict sharply (from $19 to $325 a month, with incompatible plan names) and are not used as facts here.
