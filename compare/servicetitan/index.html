Handled — AI CRM

Handled vs ServiceTitan.

ServiceTitan is the enterprise platform for larger, multi-truck operations. Handled is the agent for the one-van shop ServiceTitan prices out — with voice control of the whole app and diagnostics that show their source.

Handled is an AI CRM for small service businesses. Against ServiceTitan, its edges are voice control of the whole app, diagnostics with confirmed/unconfirmed labels, and a client card that grows itself.

The short verdict

ServiceTitan is the deepest platform of the four: dispatch, pricebook estimating, memberships, payroll, marketing attribution and inventory, with the most ambitious AI of the group in Titan Intelligence and Atlas. It is built for scale — multi-truck operations with dispatchers and call teams, per-technician pricing by quote, and a formal implementation. Handled is built for the shop ServiceTitan is not: one van, one owner, no dispatcher. Max runs that shop by voice, diagnoses from real manuals with labels ServiceTitan does not have, and grows the client card by itself.

Where Handled is different

  • Voice control of the whole app — ServiceTitan's voice AI answers customer calls; Handled lets the owner run the app by voice.
  • Diagnostics with confirmed/unconfirmed labels — Field Pro AI gives equipment and manual answers, but with no labelling, and ServiceTitan warns it "may be wrong". Handled labels every answer.
  • Photo part ID — nothing comparable was found at ServiceTitan.
  • Client card that grows itself — ServiceTitan's history is deep, but it is built by the team, not by photographing a model number or recording calls into the card.
  • Fit — Handled is van stock for a solo operator, not enterprise inventory for multi-truck operations. ServiceTitan has inventory; Handled's is the small shop's version of it, run by the same agent that books the jobs.

Where the competitor is strong

  • Depth: dispatch, pricebook estimating with regional price benchmarks, memberships, payroll and commission tracking, and marketing attribution — the most complete platform of the four.
  • Titan Intelligence and Atlas — the most ambitious AI of the four, with forecasting, benchmarking, dispatch optimisation and a conversational assistant.
  • Field Pro AI — the only partial analogue to Handled's diagnostics, giving equipment and manual answers in the field.
  • Inventory and purchasing modules with inventory-level recommendations tied to job history.

Price

ServiceTitan publishes no prices. Its pricing page lists three packages — Starter, Essentials and The Works — priced per technician, and every package shows a "Request Pricing" button with no dollar amount. No free trial was found on the pricing page. Handled's price is also TBA, so no honest price comparison can be made yet.

Third-party estimates for ServiceTitan circulate widely (commonly ~$245–$500 per technician per month, plus a one-time implementation fee) — these are not ServiceTitan's figures, they come from compilations of user reports, and they conflict with each other. They are not used as facts here. The honest comparison is fit and simplicity: ServiceTitan is built for larger operations, with annual contracts and a formal implementation; Handled is built for the small shop.
