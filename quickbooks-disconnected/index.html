You've disconnected QuickBooks

Your QuickBooks Online company is no longer connected to Nokr.

Nothing was changed or deleted inside your QuickBooks company — your invoices, customers, and payments in QuickBooks are untouched.

What happened: Nokr deleted its QuickBooks access token for your company. Nokr can no longer send invoices or payments to QuickBooks, and will not sync anything until you reconnect. Nothing was changed or deleted inside your QuickBooks company — your invoices, customers, and payments in QuickBooks are untouched. The records Nokr already synced stay in QuickBooks.

If this was a mistake, reconnect in under a minute: open the Nokr app → More → Provider hub → QuickBooks → Connect → sign in to QuickBooks and approve the connection. Your next sync will pick up where it left off and will not create duplicate invoices.
