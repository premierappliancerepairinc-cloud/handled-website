What is the free pilot?

The pilot is how Handled starts: the first ~10 companies use the product free for ~2 weeks, on real jobs, with real clients. You get Max, voice control, the schedule, the CRM, van inventory, parts search and diagnostics — and we get the feedback that shapes the product before it is sold.

There is no credit card, no contract and no obligation at the end of the pilot. If the price, when announced, does not work for you, you walk away with nothing owed.

What will the paid subscription be?

It will be announced when the Reserve with Google button works. That is the milestone the brief sets for the paid subscription, and the site will say so plainly when it happens — on this page and on the Roadmap.

What does field service software cost elsewhere?

For context, here is what the competitors publish — checked 2026-11-11. Two of the four publish no prices at all:

  • Jobber: from $29/month (Core, billed annually, 1 user).
  • Housecall Pro: from $59/month (Basic, billed annually, 1 user).
  • Workiz: no prices published — Standard, Pro and Ultimate all show "Request pricing".
  • ServiceTitan: no prices published — Starter, Essentials and The Works are priced per technician, by quote only.

One more datapoint, with its attribution: a New York appliance-repair competitor pays $700/month for Workiz with telephony — from the competitor's own words, not from Workiz's price list. Workiz publishes no prices, so this is a single business's actual bill, not a Workiz price.
