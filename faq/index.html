Answers about Handled: what it is, how Max works, what the pilot costs, when the price is announced, and what is live today.

Handled is an AI CRM for small service businesses. Max, the AI agent, takes orders, runs the schedule, writes invoices and talks to clients — the technician just repairs.

Handled is an AI CRM for small service businesses, built for appliance repair first and then for every small service business in the US. Max, the AI agent inside Handled, takes orders, runs the schedule, writes invoices and talks to clients.

Small service businesses — appliance repair first, then HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning and any one-van or small-crew shop where the owner also does the work.

Max takes orders, runs the schedule, writes invoices and talks to clients. It also controls van inventory, finds and drafts orders for parts, helps with diagnostics from real manuals, and grows the client card by itself.

You speak, and the app does it — on any tab, with no chat and no typing. Book a job, check the schedule, find a part or write an invoice by voice.

Max is trained on real service manuals. Every answer is marked confirmed — from a manual, with exact numbers and a source — or unconfirmed — an idea to verify by measurement.

Yes. Photograph a part or a nameplate and Max recognises the part number, then searches stores for it with retail and wholesale prices.

Photograph a model number and Max adds the appliance to the client card. When a client calls, Max records the call and adds it. Jobs and invoices join the card when they close. No manual entry.
