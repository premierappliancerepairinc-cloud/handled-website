What the subscription includes

One subscription covers the whole app. Here is what is in it:

  • Max — the agent that runs the CRM, finds parts, helps with diagnostics, and learns from real service manuals.
  • Voice control — run the whole app by speaking, hands free.
  • The CRM and the client card — it creates and grows itself from calls, photos and jobs.
  • Van inventory — Max remembers where every part is, down to the drawer.
  • Parts search and ordering — wholesale and retail prices at once, across your suppliers.
  • Diagnostics and photo part ID — manual-grounded answers, marked confirmed or unconfirmed, and parts identified from a photo.
  • Sofia — answers calls when you do not pick up, with your own greeting and voice.
  • Geolocation and the team map — dispatchers see where the technician is; technicians see each other.
  • Mileage and built-in accounting — mileage counted from when work starts; money on parts, sales tax, the technician and the owner, with charts.
  • Payments, booking and the client portal — live payments, online booking, and Reserve with Google.

How it works — pilot first

The subscription starts with the free pilot. The first ~10 companies use Handled free for ~2 weeks, on real jobs, with real clients. There is no credit card, no contract and no obligation at the end of the pilot. If the price, when announced, does not work for you, you walk away with nothing owed.

The paid subscription starts when the Reserve with Google button works. That is the milestone, and the price will be announced then — on this page, on Pricing, and on the Roadmap.
