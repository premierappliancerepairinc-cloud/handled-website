How Max works

Three steps, on every job. No new workflow to learn — Max fits the way a technician already works.

  1. You speak or photograph

    Say what you need, or photograph a part, a nameplate or a model number. Hands busy, driving, under a sink — it still works.

  2. Max does it

    Max books the job, updates the schedule, writes the invoice, checks the van stock, searches for the part or looks up the manual.

  3. You approve

    Nothing is ordered, sent or charged without you. Orders are drafts for your confirmation; invoices go out when you say so.

Who Handled is for

Handled is built for appliance repair first — the trade it was made in — and then for every small service business in the United States: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, and any one-van or small-crew shop where the owner also does the work.

If you are the technician, the dispatcher, the bookkeeper and the person who answers the phone, Handled is made for you. Max takes the office work; you take the repair.

Why Handled

Three things no competitor was found to offer, checked 2026-11-11:

  • Voice control of the whole app — not voice for one feature, but speak-and-the-app-does-it on any tab.
  • Diagnostics from real service manuals, with every answer marked confirmed or unconfirmed — so you know what is a fact and what is an idea to verify.
  • A client card that grows itself — from a photographed model number and from recorded calls, with no manual entry.
