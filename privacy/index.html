Privacy policy

What this site collects

The pilot form collects your name, business name, email and phone number — only what you type into it — so we can contact you about a pilot spot. Nothing else is collected by this site. This site uses no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics.

How it is used

Your details are used only to answer your pilot request and to contact you about Handled. They are not sold, rented or shared with advertisers. If you ask us to delete your details, we will.

Contact

To ask about your details, or to ask for them to be deleted, use the pilot form on the Pricing page and say so in the message field.
