Nokr — AI CRM

Privacy policy.

What the Nokr app and this website collect, what we do with it, and what we never do.

No tracking across other apps or websites. No ads. No sale of personal data. No use of your data to train AI models.

Last updated: November 19, 2026.

Nokr is an app for small service businesses (appliance repair first, then other field-service trades) to run their work: customers, jobs, schedules, invoices, payments, and parts. It is provided by Badma Kharashikov, an individual doing business as Nokr ("Nokr", "we", "us"), based in New York, USA. This policy explains what the app and this website collect, what we do with it, and what we never do.

What we collect

Account data. Company name, owner name, email address, and phone number — provided by you at registration.

Business data. The customers, jobs, invoices, schedules, messages, and photos you add. This data belongs to your company and is stored on your company's server account. It is used only to run your business in the app. Other companies on Nokr cannot see it.

Voice audio. When you talk to Dana, the voice assistant, your audio is sent to your company's server and transcribed there by a speech model running on that server. The audio file is deleted immediately after transcription. The transcribed text is used only to carry out your request.

Photos. Photos you take in the app (for example, of an appliance model number) are stored with your company's jobs.

Location. Only if you turn on team location sharing. It is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time.

Call and message records. If you use Nokr's phone service, the calls and messages you make and receive through your Nokr business number are logged (number, time, duration) so you can see them in the app. Calls may be recorded; a recording notice is played at the start of a call where the law requires it.

Payment data (Nokr Payments). For each payment, Nokr stores the amount, date, status, the card's brand and last 4 digits, and the payout status — as reported by Stripe. Nokr never sees or stores full card numbers, card security codes (CVV/CVC), card expiry dates, or full bank account numbers. Those are entered on Stripe's own pages (or tapped to the phone through Stripe Terminal) and are held by Stripe, not by Nokr.

QuickBooks data. If you connect QuickBooks, Nokr sends your customers, invoices, payments, and item names to your QuickBooks Online company so your books match your work, and reads back only what it needs to avoid duplicates (record IDs and invoice numbers). Nokr stores the QuickBooks IDs of the records it created and the result of the last sync. Nokr never reads your books, never changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks, and never sends your QuickBooks data to anyone else. You can disconnect QuickBooks at any time in the app; disconnecting deletes Nokr's QuickBooks access token.

Crash diagnostics. If the app crashes, an error report (error text, app version, screen name) is sent to the server so the bug can be fixed. It contains no device identifier and no business data. Secrets such as passwords and keys are removed from reports before they are sent.

Website pilot form. This website's pilot form collects your name, business name, email, and phone number — only what you type into it — so we can contact you about a pilot spot. This site uses no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics.

What we never do

No tracking across other apps or websites. No advertising identifiers (no IDFA). No ads. No sale of personal data. No access to your phone contacts. No use of your data to train AI models. No use of one company's data to benefit another company.

Who else receives data

Anthropic (Claude). Dana, the in-app assistant, is powered by Anthropic's Claude. Your messages to Dana and the business context needed to answer them are sent to Anthropic under Anthropic's privacy policy. Audio is never sent to Anthropic — it is transcribed on your server first. Data read back from QuickBooks is never sent to Anthropic.

Stripe. When your company uses Nokr Payments, the data Stripe needs to process payments is shared with Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. Nokr never holds your customers' money: payments go to your own Nokr Payments Account with Stripe.

Twilio and SendGrid. If you use Nokr's phone service, calls and messages go through Twilio (voice/SMS) and SendGrid (email) under their privacy policies. Your business phone number comes from Nokr's pool.

Providers you connect. If you connect QuickBooks, Google Business Profile, or a parts supplier, the data needed for that connection is shared with that provider under its own policy. Nothing is shared with them unless you connect them. Nokr shares data with Google only as needed for bookings, and only if you enable Google booking.

DigitalOcean. Your company's data is stored on a server hosted by DigitalOcean in Toronto, Canada.

Where your data lives

Your company's data is stored on a server hosted by DigitalOcean in Toronto, Canada. Your data is yours: other companies on Nokr cannot see it, and Nokr sees only aggregate counts (for example, how many companies are active) — never the content of your jobs, customers, or messages.

How long we keep data

Voice audio is deleted right after transcription. Payment records are kept while your account is active, because you need them for your own books and tax records. Everything else is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, you have 30 days to export your data; after that, your data is permanently deleted, except for Nokr's own billing records, which we keep as required by law.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and data in the app. To ask about your data, or to ask for it to be deleted, write to getnokr@gmail.com.

Children

Nokr is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects your data, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. Material changes to payment data practices require a new version of the Merchant Agreement and fresh acceptance.
